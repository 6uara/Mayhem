# Music credits

## Placeholder tracks (current)

`menu.wav`, `combat.wav`, `shop.wav` are **procedurally synthesized placeholders**,
generated by `tools/generate_placeholder_music.py` (stdlib-only Python, fixed RNG
seed, deterministic re-runs) — same reasoning as
`tools/generate_placeholder_sfx.py` for every SFX in the game. Not licensed from
anywhere; not composed by a person. No attribution required because there is no
third-party material here yet.

**Replace this section with real credits the moment licensed or composed tracks
land** — whoever does that pass must list, per track: title, composer/licensor,
license (e.g. CC-BY, a purchased license, an original commission), and any
attribution text the license requires to ship in-game or in credits.
